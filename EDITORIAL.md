## Editorial and mathematical changes

- Merge the repeated related-vector-field lemma; replace stale lecture-number references with semantic cross-references.
- Expand completeness, direct-sum chart, and connected-generation arguments.
- Prove smoothness using the differential of multiplication and the smooth flow theorem.
- Distinguish the embedded subgroup argument from the integral-leaf input for immersed Lie subgroups.
- State precisely that the subgroup interval is nonempty; reduce an arbitrary interval to one around zero.
- Treat the constant term separately in Hilbert–Schmidt estimates: its norm is square root of n, not 1.
- Fix a clear convention for conjugate transpose and the complex inner product.
- Give the skew-symmetric necessity by differentiation rather than implying global injectivity of exp.
- Keep continuous one-parameter subgroup regularity explicitly outside the proofs given here.
- Add explanatory examples, the unitary case, and six original supplementary exercises; these are labeled in the text.
